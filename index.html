About Us

Muhabura Shine Secondary School is a leading day and boarding secondary school located along Kisoro-Bunagana Road in Kisoro Municipality. We are committed to providing holistic, affordable and quality education inspired by discipline, hard work and God-fearing values, under the shadow of Mt. Muhabura.

🎓 Our Mission

To provide quality education that nurtures academic excellence, moral integrity and self-reliance.

👁️ Our Vision

To be a centre of academic excellence shining in Kisoro District and Uganda at large.

📚 Academics

Senior 1 - Senior 6 (Arts & Sciences), UNEB Registered, Experienced Teachers, Computer Lab, Science Labs.

🏫 Why Us?

Disciplined Environment, Affordable Fees, Boarding Facilities, Co-curricular Activities, Spiritual Growth.
